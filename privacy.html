Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

Galaxy Academy ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website and attendance management platform. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect student and parent information when using our service or interacting with us via WhatsApp.

1. Information We Collect

  • Student & Parent Information: Names, roll numbers, registered phone numbers, and class attendance/test mark records.
  • WhatsApp Communication Data: Mobile phone number, profile name, timestamp, and contents of messages sent to our WhatsApp Business endpoint for attendance notifications and parent query processing.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information strictly for educational and administrative purposes:

  • Sending attendance notifications, absentee reports, and test score updates to parents.
  • Processing parent responses and queries via WhatsApp.
  • Managing academy fee payment records and student performance history.

3. Data Protection and Sharing

We prioritize your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or trade student or parent personal information to third parties. Data is shared only with trusted infrastructure providers (such as Meta Cloud API for WhatsApp messaging and Google Firebase for secure data storage) strictly to operate the service.

4. Data Security

We implement administrative and technical security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
